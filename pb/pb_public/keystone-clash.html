Tournament info

Everything in the coaches packet, plus the parking map. Please pass the parking notes on to your families.

Both lots are marked in orange. Enter off Meadow St. Overflow parking is on East O’Hara St.

Where
East End Park, 51 Meadow St, McDonald, PA 15057. Two fields.
Parking
Two small lots, and both fill fast. Overflow is on East O’Hara St, a short walk to the fields. Arrive early and keep driveways and outfield access lanes clear.
Rules
USA Softball rules. 90 minutes, finish the inning. Two umpires every game. Courtesy runner for the pitcher and catcher. Run rule 12 after 3, 10 after 4, 8 after 5. Teams may bat the whole lineup or follow USA Softball substitution rules — declare at the plate meeting. A game is official after three full innings; if rain stops play mid-inning, the score reverts to the last completed inning.
Full Rules
Format
Pool play Friday evening and Saturday morning. Seeds post early Saturday afternoon and bracket play begins right after — plan to stay at the complex. Semifinals and the championship are Sunday.
On site
Porta johns and hand sanitizing stations all weekend. Concessions are run by the Fort Cherry Fastpitch Association.
Questions
Derek Rocco, Artie Tortorice and Steve LaFever, Tournament Directors. Text both teams’ final scores after every game.
